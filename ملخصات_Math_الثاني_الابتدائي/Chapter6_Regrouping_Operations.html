Chapter 6: Addition & Subtraction with Regrouping

Carrying Over, Borrowing, and Real-Life Word Problems

1

Part 1: Concept Explanations & Visual Rules

🎈 Regrouping Rule: If the sum in the Ones place is 10 or more, carry 1 over to the Tens place! If you cannot subtract, borrow 1 Ten (10 ones).

1. 2-Digit & 3-Digit Addition with Regrouping

Example: 47 + 28
• Add ones: 7 + 8 = 15 (Write 5, carry 1 to tens).
• Add tens: 1 + 4 + 2 = 7. Result = 75.

2. Subtraction with Regrouping (Borrowing)

Example: 52 - 19
• 2 - 9? We cannot! Borrow 1 from 5 tens (becomes 4 tens).
• The 2 becomes 12. Now 12 - 9 = 3.
• Subtract tens: 4 - 1 = 3. Result = 33.
