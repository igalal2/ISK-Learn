🧠Mental Math Secrets (استراتيجيات الحساب الذهني)

Strategy 1: Making Tens / Friendly Numbers (تكوين العشرات):

Look for friendly pairs that make 10: (1+9), (2+8), (3+7), (4+6), (5+5).
Example from book illustration: (2 + 8) + 11 = 10 + 11 = 21. It's much faster to add 2+8 first!

Strategy 2: Associative Property of Addition (خاصية الدمج):

Changing the grouping of addends does not change the sum:
(2 + 8) + 11 = 2 + (8 + 11) = 21.

Strategy 3: Doubles & Near Doubles (المضاعفات):

6 + 6 = 12 ➔ therefore 6 + 7 = 12 + 1 = 13!
