🧃Understanding Capacity (مفهوم السعة ووحداتها)

What is Capacity? (ما هي السعة؟):

Capacity is the amount of liquid a container can hold (كمية السائل التي يتسع لها الوعاء).

The 3 Units of Capacity in Book:

• Liter (L / لتر): for large amounts (bottle of water, juice carton, bucket).
• Deciliter (dL / ديسيلتر): for medium cups (1 dL = 100 mL, 1 L = 10 dL).
• Milliliter (mL / ملليلتر): for very small amounts (medicine spoon, eye drops, perfume spray).
• Key Conversion: 1 Liter (L) = 1,000 Milliliters (mL).
