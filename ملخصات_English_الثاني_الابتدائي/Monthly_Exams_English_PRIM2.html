🇬🇧Connect 2 - October Monthly Exam (Units 1 & 2)
El-Moasser & Ministry Specs • Total: 20 Marks
1. Look and complete: (father, grandparents, cousin):

"These are my grandmother and grandfather. They are my ................"

A) father
B) grandparents ⭐
C) cousin
✅ Model Answer: B) grandparents
📌 Explanation: Grandfather + grandmother = grandparents (الجد والجدة).
2. Irregular Plural: Choose the correct word: "I have one foot, but two ................"
A) foots
B) feet ⭐
C) tooth
✅ Model Answer: B) feet
📌 Explanation: Foot is an irregular plural: 1 foot ➔ 2 feet (قدم ➔ أقدام).
