Part 1: Key Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

💡 Grammar & Science Tip: The food moves from the esophagus to the stomach! The stomach churns food with gastric juice to give us energy and nutrients!

1. The Human Digestive System

• Mouth: We chew food with our teeth and mix it with saliva.
• Esophagus: Tube that moves food from mouth to stomach.
• Stomach: Gastric juice breaks down food into simpler forms.
• Small & Large Intestines: Small intestine absorbs nutrients; large intestine takes water/salts and removes waste.

2. The Respiratory System

• We breathe in oxygen through our nose and mouth.
• The diaphragm moves down and pulls air into the lungs.
• In the lungs, blood absorbs oxygen and pumps it around the body.
• The diaphragm moves up to push out carbon dioxide.

3. Language Focus: Conjunctions (and / but)

• Use and to add similar ideas: "I eat fruit, and I exercise."
• Use but to show contrast: "I want to play football, but I hurt my leg."
