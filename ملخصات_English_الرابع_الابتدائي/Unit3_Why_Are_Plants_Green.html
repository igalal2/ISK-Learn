Plant Cells, Seed Germination, Habitats, and Superlative Adjectives

1

Part 1: Key Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

💡 Superlative Rule: The + short adjective + -est (e.g. The Nile is the longest river). The most + long adjective (e.g. The sunflower is the most beautiful flower).

1. Plant Germination & Cells

• We put a seed in soil, water it, and sunlight helps it grow (Germination).
• Roots grow downward under the soil; shoot grows upward toward the sun.
• Plant cells are tiny and contain chloroplasts (which contain chlorophyll making plants green).

2. Plant Habitats in Egypt

• Agricultural Habitat: Lotus flower, reeds, and orange trees near the River Nile.
• Desert Habitat: Acacia tree, tamarisk (they don't need much water).
