Theme 1: I Discover Myself (Units 1, 2 & 3)

Food & Farming, Desert Animals, and Why Plants Are Green • Illustrated Vocabulary & Grammar

1

Part 1: Key Vocabulary & Grammar Concepts

💡 Teacher's Golden Grammar Tip: When comparing 2 animals: Short adjective + ER + THAN (e.g. A camel is taller than a horse). For long adjectives: MORE / LESS + adjective + THAN (e.g. A snake is more dangerous than a fox).

Unit 1: I feel good! (Food, Body & Digestion)

• Key Words: chicken, rice, watermelon, digestive system, respiratory system, stomach, lungs, heart.
• Phonics: Prefix 're-' means to do again (repaint, rewrite, reclean).

Unit 2: Desert Animals (Adaptation & Wildlife)

• Animals: Fennec fox (huge ears, thick fur), Camel (flat feet, hump for fat), Steppe eagle, Pelican.
• Grammar: Comparatives: faster than, bigger than, heavier than.

Unit 3: Why are plants green?

• Concepts: Seeds, germination, roots, shoot, leaves, pollen, chloroplasts (make plants green).
• Grammar: Superlatives: The + adjective + EST (The biggest, the tallest).

Part 3: Model Answers

  • 2. taller (comparative with 'than').
