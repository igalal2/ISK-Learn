Unit 5: Where Do You Work? • Connect 4

Community Helpers, Tools, Jobs, and Job Safety

1

Part 1: Key Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

💡 Job Pronouns: Who connects pipes? A plumber. Who fixes electricity? An electrician. Who carries letters? A mail carrier!

1. Community Helpers & Their Jobs

• Electrician: Connects electricity so we can use lights and TV. Uses a screwdriver and avoids touching wet wires.
• Plumber: Connects pipes and faucets so we have clean water to wash and drink.
• Garbage Collector: Keeps our city clean and takes trash for recycling.
• Mail Carrier: Delivers letters, packages, and bills all day long.

2. Electrical Safety Tips

• Never put anything metal into a socket.
• Never touch electrical appliances with wet hands.
• Turn off switches when leaving the room.
