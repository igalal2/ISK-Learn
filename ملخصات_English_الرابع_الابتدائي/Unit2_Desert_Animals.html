Unit 2: Desert Animals • Connect 4

Camels, Fennec Fox, Steppe Eagle, Rhim Gazelle, and Comparative Adjectives

1

Part 1: Key Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

💡 Comparative Rule: Short adjective + -er + than (e.g. The camel is taller than the horse). Long adjective: more + adjective + than (e.g. The snake is more dangerous than the fox).

1. Amazing Desert Animals

• Fennec Fox: Has huge ears to hear insects and lose heat, lives in sand dunes.
• Camel: Has large flat feet so it doesn't sink in sand, stores fat in its hump, can live without water for a long time.
• Steppe Eagle: Flies across Europe to Africa, eats small mammals, has amazing eyesight.
• Rhim Gazelle: Has big ears, long horns, and yellow-white coat to reflect desert sun.

2. Comparative Adjectives

• Fast -> faster than | Big -> bigger than | Heavy -> heavier than.
• Beautiful -> more beautiful than | Dangerous -> more dangerous than.
